Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. The app has no account and does not upload your entries anywhere. Tools > Data > Erase all data removes everything the app has stored.

How do I try the app with sample data?

Open Tools, tap the Data icon at the top right, and tap Load sample data in the Sample data section. The button works when the app has no records yet. To start over, tap Erase all data first.

How do I move my data to another iPhone?

On the first iPhone, open Tools and tap the share icon at the top right. Choose the sections to include, enter a passphrase of at least 8 characters, tap Create file, and then tap Share with AirDrop or choose another app.

On the second iPhone, open the file, choose Paycheck Expenses, enter the same passphrase, and tap Add to my data. The app adds only the records that the device does not have yet.

I forgot the passphrase. Can you open the file?

No. The app does not store passphrases, and the developer cannot recover them. Create a new file with a new passphrase.

Why does the app ask for notifications?

Only for renewal reminders for subscriptions and for the parking timer. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. When the device is online, the app also shows two public figures: the average 30-year mortgage rate and the consumer price change over the last 12 months. Without a connection, it shows the figures it saved earlier, or hides those cards if it has none.

Where do the mortgage rate and inflation figures come from?

The mortgage rate is the weekly average from the Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey. The price change is the 12-month change in the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index for all urban consumers (CPI-U). The figures are for information only. Your own loan rate may differ.

Does the app send information about me?

No. The app only asks for the two public figures. As with any internet request, the servers can see the IP address of your device, but the app sends none of your entries.

The take-home estimate does not match my paystub.

The estimate uses 2025 federal tax brackets for a single filer with the standard deduction, and approximate flat state rates. Your employer's payroll uses your actual withholding. Use the estimate for planning, and check your paystub for exact figures.

Which currency and units does the app use?

US dollars, miles, gallons, pounds and degrees Fahrenheit. The app is available in English.
