Privacy Policy

Paycheck Expenses

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Developer: Alex Tilly

Contact: tilly_all@icloud.com

Summary

Paycheck Expenses does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your data stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). The app works fully without an internet connection.

When your device is online, the app downloads two public U.S. figures from official government sources: the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate (Freddie Mac) and the consumer price change over the last 12 months (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics). These requests contain no personal information.

Information you enter

You can enter salary and deduction amounts, your bank balance, expenses, budgets, subscriptions, vehicles, fuel and service records, savings goals and the inputs of the tools (for example bill amounts or loan terms). This information is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. The developer cannot access it.

Public figures from the internet

The app makes two read-only requests to fixed addresses: one to Freddie Mac for weekly mortgage rates and one to the Bureau of Labor Statistics for the consumer price index. The app checks for new figures only while the device is online. After it gets both figures, it waits a day before checking again. If a check does not get both figures, for example because the device was offline, the app tries again the next time you open it. The requests do not include your name, your entries or any identifier of yours. Like any internet request, the servers can see the IP address of your device, and the request header contains the app's name and version. The app saves the two figures on your device. Without a connection, it shows the figures it saved earlier, or hides those cards if it has none.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders for subscription renewals and for the parking timer. These reminders are created and delivered on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Files you choose to share

Tools > Share data creates a file that contains the data you select. The file is encrypted with a passphrase that you choose. The app does not store the passphrase. You send the file with the iOS share sheet, for example with AirDrop or Files, to a destination you choose. The developer does not receive the file.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage data or diagnostics. The app does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your information

  • Tools > Data > Erase all data removes everything the app has stored.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it has stored.
  • Copies of files you shared are outside the app. Delete them where you sent them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the effective date above will be updated.
